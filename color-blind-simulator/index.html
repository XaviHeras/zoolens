This is a simulation, not a test or a diagnosis. If you think you might be colour-blind, see an optician or eye doctor.

Question 1

Protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia: what’s the difference?

They are named after the cone that’s missing. Protanopia (red-blind): no red cones, so reds turn dark and close to green. Deuteranopia (green-blind): no green cones, so reds and greens look alike. Tritanopia (blue-blind): no blue cones, so blues and yellows change the most. Milder forms, where a cone works differently rather than being missing, are much more common.

Question 2

How common is colour blindness?

About 1 in 100 men is red-blind, and about 1 in 100 is green-blind. Milder red-green forms are much more common: a mild green form alone affects about 5 in 100 men. Blue-blindness is rare, with estimates from 1 in 10,000 to 1 in 65,000 people, and seeing no colour at all affects about 1 in 30,000.

Question 3

How does a traffic light look?

In the simulation, the red and green lights come out close in colour for red-blind and green-blind eyes; what still differs is which lamp is lit. Try the camera on a traffic light near you, or switch the sample to the pie chart.

Question 4

What about seeing no colour at all?

That’s achromatopsia. Pick No colour to see only light and dark. Real achromatopsia also brings less sharp sight and discomfort in bright light; the simulation shows only the colour.

Question 5

Is my slide readable?

Take a photo of your slide or chart, or point the camera at the screen, and switch between Red-blind and Green-blind. If two parts of a chart differ only by red and green, they may look alike: add labels or patterns, or make one lighter. The pie chart sample shows how it goes. Pick Side by side to see the whole picture twice, as most people see it and simulated, next to each other.

How this works

The demo uses the same colour maths as Zoolens. Red-blind, green-blind and blue-blind come from a published model of colour vision deficiency (Machado and others, 2009), applied in linear light; No colour keeps only the brightness. Everything happens in this tab: once the demo has started, you can turn on airplane mode and it still works.
