Zoolens press kit

Zoolens is an iPhone camera app that shows the world live as 39 animals might see it, from a dog to a mantis shrimp, plus 12 kinds of human sight, such as colour blindness or a baby’s eyes. Coming soon to the App Store.

How the science works

Each effect is a simulation based on vision research: the colours come from published models, the sharpness from published measurements where they exist, and every page on this site cites its sources. What a camera can’t capture, like ultraviolet, heat or polarised light, is estimated from ordinary colour, and the bat’s sonar is imagined. The app says so for each animal.

Story ideas

  • Through a mantis shrimp’s eyes: 12 colour channels, yet worse than us at telling close colours apart.
  • Beyond the dog filter: 39 animals in one camera.
  • Empathy through a camera: how a colour-blind person or a baby might see, free.
  • Made by one person, with no ads or tracking.
